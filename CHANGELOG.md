# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Items you still need for a quest you haven't finished get a little quest icon in your backpack, your bank and shops.
- Hover the icon to see which quests want the item.
- Pick which kinds of quest to mark (quests, miniquests, sagas and so on) from the ribbon panel.
- Finished quests get markers too: green means hang on to it, an achievement still needs it. Red means you're done with it and it's safe to clear out.
- Shops leave the red markers off the Buy tab, since you don't own those. The Sell tab still shows them.
- Don't want the green or red ones? Untick them in the panel.
- You can bind a key to show or hide all the markers.
- Quest data comes from the RuneScape Wiki.
