# Quest Item Marker

Puts a small quest icon on items you'll need for quests you haven't finished yet. You'll see it in your backpack, your bank and in shops.

## What the icons mean

- **Plain quest icon:** a quest you haven't finished still needs this item. Keep it.
- **Green icon:** the quest is done, but an achievement still uses the item. Keep it until you've done that achievement.
- **Red icon:** every quest and achievement that used this item is finished. You can bank it, sell it or drop it.

Hover over any icon to see which quests or achievements it's for.

## Shops

Icons show on shop items too, so you can spot a quest item you still need to buy. Red icons stay off the Buy tab, since there's no point telling you you're done with something you don't own. They still show on the Sell tab.

## Settings

Open the plugin from the ribbon to change:

- **Mark items needed for:** quests, miniquests, or both.
- **For finished quests, also mark:** turn the green or red icons on or off.
- **Shortcut to show or hide markers:** pick a key to hide all the icons and bring them back. There's no shortcut until you set one.
