Quest Item Marker: third-party notices

Quest Item Marker's code is written independently. The bundled reference data in
data/quest_items.lua and data/post_quest_items.lua is derived from the sources below.
They are generated by tools/build_quest_items.py and tools/build_post_quest.py; the
hand-reviewed fixes they apply are in tools/data/ (not shipped), and
data/build_report.txt and data/post_quest_report.txt record each build.

Derived from the Wiki (Bucket and MediaWiki APIs, retrieved 2026-11-16 with the
User-Agent "QuestItemMarker-build/0.1 (https://github.com/DuncanLevings/rs3-quest-item-marker;
Discord: Tatsuya)"):

- Each quest's "Items required" list, pinned to the quest page's revision (the
  revision ID is stored with every quest in the data file).
  https://runescape.wiki/w/Bucket:Quest
- Item IDs and item versions for every linked item page.
  https://runescape.wiki/w/Bucket:Infobox_item
- Quest categories (quest, miniquest, seasonal, tutorial) from Wiki category
  membership, and the item pages listed on disambiguation pages.

Changes made by Quest Item Marker: the requirement text is reduced to item IDs,
amounts and "any one of" groups. Lines saying an item is given during the quest,
or is optional, are left out, as are items a clause says won't work. Links to
people, places, skills and similar are dropped. Some broad pages ("any light
source", "any hatchet") are expanded to a hand-picked list of items, and some
("any weapon", "any food") are left out; tools/data/link_classes.json lists each
decision. No Wiki prose is bundled.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Jagex
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Quest ids and config names, item ids and the quest icon sprite are RuneScape client
identifiers (Jagex Ltd.), read in game by the author. Only the identifiers Quest
Item Marker uses are kept, and no game images are distributed.
