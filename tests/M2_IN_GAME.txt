Quest Item Marker - M2 (backpack) in-game test
==============================================

What M2 covers: quest status (read once at login, then kept fresh by quest
variable events), icons and hover tooltips on the backpack, the ribbon settings
panel, and the on/off checkbox plus shortcut. Bank and shops come in M3 and M4.

Automated checks (no client): from the plugin folder run

    lua tests/run.lua

It should end with "51 passed, 0 failed". Those tests use a fake client, so they
don't prove anything about the real one. That's what the steps below are for.

Write down what you see at each "Check", and copy the [Quest Item Marker] lines from
the console (Alt + `) into your notes. The console keeps only about 1000 lines.


0. Set up
---------
1. Turn the M0 probe off if it's on (it draws its own test icons).
2. In the console: prf, then pon prettyui-dev (if it isn't on already), then
   pon quest-item-marker. After an edit, use prl quest-item-marker to reload.
3. Check: the console shows
     data loaded: snapshot 2026-11-16 | 227 quests | ... 0 bad quests, 0 bad needs skipped
     started | markers on
   and, once you're in game,
     quest status read: 227 quests | N with no watchable variable ... | M unknown to this client
     Backpack: OK, <k> marked
   WRITE DOWN N and M. (N is the design doc's open count of quests with no varPlayer.
   M should be 0.)
4. Open the plugin's ribbon panel.
   Check: only the title, "Show quest item markers" (ticked) and the shortcut field.
5. Optional: allow the plugin's chat permission (chatAccess) in the Plugin Hub. Without
   it, short messages ("Markers hidden") only go to the console.


1. Icons and tooltips on the sample quests (CUJ 1)
-------------------------------------------------
Put items from the M1 sample quests in your backpack (from the bank, or with item
cheats on the beta client), plus some junk. For each marked item, hover it.

Check for every item:
  - The blue quest-list icon (RS3_ICON_QUEST_LIST) in the top-right corner, only on
    items an unfinished quest needs. Junk and empty slots have none.
  - The tooltip appears only while the mouse is on the ICON, not the rest of the slot.
    Clicking the icon still clicks the item.
  - The tooltip: one line per quest, name in light grey and the hovered item's amount
    in soft yellow ("+N more" in a darker grey), the amount only when it's 2 or more (never "x1"), quests in progress
    first, at most 5 then "+N more". Other choices are never named. Items that must
    come WITH the hovered one show on a second line ("+ Vial of water"). No header,
    states or footer, no box characters.
  - Each quest line starts with a small blue quest icon; a long quest name wraps
    under it, and the next quest's icon still lines up with its own line. The
    "+ Vial of water" and "+N more" lines have no icon.
  - Quest box spot: with nothing saved, the quest list shows centred near the top of
    the screen, wherever the item is. It disappears when the mouse leaves the icon.
  - In the settings panel, press "Move quest box". A "Quest box" window opens at the
    current spot. Drag it by its title somewhere else, close it, and hover a marker:
    the list's top-left corner is where the window's was. Try it with the game's
    interface lock on too. Reload the plugin: the spot is kept.
  - Drop the window near the bottom-right corner and hover an item with a long list:
    the list stays fully on screen (moved up and left as needed).
  - Press "Move quest box" twice: still one window. Unload the plugin with it open:
    it goes away.
  - Left-click, right-click menus and the game's own hover text still work on marked
    items.
  - Compare the quest list with the quest's Wiki "Items required" (snapshot
    2026-11-16). A quest you've finished must never appear.

Sample quests and what to expect:
  - Priest in Peril: rune essence and pure essence (also noted) show
    "Priest in Peril x50" (rune essence) or "Priest in Peril x25" (pure), and never
    name the other essence. Rune
    essence also lists The Slug Menace and Garden of Tranquillity if unfinished.
    Buckets, vials, jugs, bowls and empty pots are never marked (containers count as
    common), with or without water in them.
  - Cook's Assistant: nothing (every line is "obtained during the quest"). Its items
    may still be marked for *other* quests; the tooltip must not name Cook's Assistant.
  - Dimension of Disaster and every DoD subquest: never named in any tooltip (left out
    on purpose). A staff of fire, for example, is only marked if another quest needs it.
  - Glorious Memories: a staff of earth is marked, and its tooltip shows just the quest
    name. Earth runes themselves: no icon, no tooltip.
  - Doric's Task V (a miniquest): each bar shows "Doric's Task V (miniquest)" with its
    own amount.
  - Heroes' Quest, Throne of Miscellania (rings, logs, iron bar), The Fremennik Isles
    (tin ore x8, coal x7, mithril ore x6), Curse of the Black Stone (rope; its law
    rune has no icon), Murder on the Border (amounts like x24 and x144),
    Goblin Diplomacy (a dye shows just the quest; onions show "Goblin Diplomacy x2"
    and "+ Coins x5").
  - Hunt for Red Raktuber: a plank shows "Hunt for Red Raktuber x5" (1 for the
    clockwork suit plus 4), on one line.
  - A Guild of Our Own (miniquest): a clean irit shows "A Guild of Our Own
    (miniquest)" and "+ Vial of water"; an unfinished irit potion shows just the quest.

Common items: coins, hammer, tinderbox and the standard runes.
  Check: no icon, and hovering shows only the game's own hover text (no plugin
  tooltip at all).


2. Quest progress (CUJ 4) - the VarTransmit check for the design doc
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Hold an item for a quest you haven't started (Priest in Peril's essence is easy:
   talk to King Roald in Varrock to start it).
2. Do one quest step (starting the quest counts).
   Check, within a few seconds:
     [Quest Item Marker] quest variable <id> changed <a> -> <b> | re-reading <quest>
     [Quest Item Marker] <quest> is now in_progress (was not_started)
   and the quest moves to the top of the tooltip. "Quest reads since login" (logged on
   the next toggle) went up by a small number (one per quest re-read), not by 227.
3. Do a further step that doesn't change the state.
   Check: the "quest variable ... changed" line appears again (no "is now" line).
4. Finish a quest whose item you're holding, if you can (any short quest works).
   Check: a "quest variable" line, "<quest> is now finished", and within a few
   seconds the icon goes from that item if no other unfinished quest needs it, or the
   quest leaves its tooltip.
WRITE DOWN: did the variable line appear on each step, and on finishing? If a step
gave no line, note the quest. That quest's progress isn't being watched.


3. Show/hide (CUJ 5)
--------------------
1. In the ribbon panel, click the shortcut field and press a key (for example F9).
2. Press the shortcut 10+ times quickly, ending with markers shown. Try it once while
   typing in the chat box too.
   Check: icons and any tooltip vanish and come back at once (no delay on turning on);
   "Markers hidden" / "Markers shown" in chat (with chatAccess) or the console; the
   checkbox follows; typing in chat doesn't toggle; every console line
     markers on|off | quest reads since login: R
   shows the SAME R.
3. Untick the checkbox.
   Check: no icons; hovering a quest item shows only the game's text; clicks work;
   the console says "Backpack: off".
4. Reload the plugin (prl quest-item-marker).
   Check: markers are still off after the reload. Tick the box again.


4. Screen changes
-----------------
1. Move a marked item to another slot, drop a marked item, pick it back up.
   Check: the icon follows the item within a second; no icon left on an empty slot.
   Picking up or dropping an item never makes the OTHER icons blink.
2. Resize or move the backpack window (or switch interface layout if you use one).
   Check: icons stay in the corner of their items.
3. Close and reopen the backpack.
   Check: icons back on reopen. (Opening is when unwatched quests are re-read: at most
   once every ~30 seconds; R goes up by at most N per 30 seconds.)
4. Teleport somewhere.
   Check: icons are back within a second of arriving (they may blink).
5. Log out to the lobby and back in.
   Check: no icons until a moment after login; then the same icons as before.
   The "quest status read" line appears again.


5. Shutdown (zero leftovers)
----------------------------
1. With icons showing, hover one item so its tooltip is up, then turn the plugin off
   in the Plugin Hub (or reload it with prl quest-item-marker).
   Check: every icon and the tooltip disappear; the console shows "stopped".
2. With the plugin off, hover and click the items that had icons.
   Check: only the game's own hover text, clicks and menus work.
3. Turn it on, off and on again three times, then look at the backpack.
   Check: exactly one icon per marked item (no doubles), tooltips still right.


Send back: N and M from step 0, the console lines from part 2, anything that looked
wrong, and anything that felt slow.
