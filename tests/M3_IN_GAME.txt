Quest Item Marker - M3 (bank) in-game test
==========================================

What M3 covers: quest icons and the same hover tooltips in the bank (placeholders
too) and on the backpack panel inside the bank screen. Icons follow tab switches,
searches, scrolling and reordering in the same frame. Shops come in M4, the quest
type filter in M5.

Automated checks (no client): from the plugin folder run

    lua tests/run.lua

It should end with "62 passed, 0 failed". Those tests use a fake client, so they
don't prove anything about the real one. That's what the steps below are for.

Write down what you see at each "Check", and copy the [Quest Item Marker] lines from
the console (Alt + `) into your notes.


0. Set up
---------
1. Reload with prl quest-item-marker (prettyui-dev must be on).
2. Make sure bank placeholders are on (bank settings), and that the bank holds items
   from a few unfinished quests (the M2 sample quests work: rune or pure essence for
   Priest in Peril, planks, ores, a dye) plus some junk.
3. Have at least one placeholder for a quest item: withdraw ALL of a marked item so
   its placeholder stays behind.
4. Put one marked item in your backpack too.


1. Opening the bank (CUJ 2)
---------------------------
1. Open the bank.
   Check: the console shows
     Bank: OK, <k> marked
     Bank backpack: OK, <j> marked
   Icons appear in the top-right corner of every bank item an unfinished quest needs,
   and on the marked item in the bank's own backpack panel. Junk, empty slots and
   common items (coins, runes, hammer, containers) have none.
2. Check: the placeholder from step 0.3 has an icon too, and hovering it shows the
   same quest list as the item would.
3. Hover a bank icon.
   Check: the quest list shows at the quest box spot (set with "Move quest box"),
   ABOVE the bank window, never under it. It goes when the mouse leaves the icon.
   Clicks, right-click menus and the game's hover text on bank items still work.
4. Compare a few bank tooltips with the quest's Wiki "Items required". A finished
   quest must never appear.


2. Moving around the bank (CUJ 2) - watch for icons lagging or left behind
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each step, watch the icons WHILE the bank changes. An icon must move with its
item in the same frame, and no icon may sit on an empty spot or on the wrong item.
1. Switch through every bank tab, including the "all items" tab.
2. Scroll the bank up and down, slowly and quickly.
3. Search for a marked item's name, then for junk, then clear the search.
   Check: only the matching items keep icons; they come back on clearing.
4. Drag a marked item to another slot, and into another tab.
5. Deposit a marked item from your backpack, then withdraw one (and all) of one.
   Check: the icon follows it within a second; withdrawing all leaves the icon on its
   placeholder; the backpack panel's icons update too.
6. Deposit all (backpack) with marked items in it.
   Check: no icon is left floating; the right items end up marked.
WRITE DOWN anything that lingered, blinked a lot or landed on the wrong item, and in
which step.


3. Closing the bank
-------------------
1. Close the bank (Esc or the close button).
   Check: every bank icon goes at once; the console shows
     Bank: closed (...)
     Bank backpack: closed (...)
   The normal backpack keeps its icons.
2. Open the bank again, then close it by walking away or teleporting.
   Check: no bank icon stays on screen anywhere afterwards.
3. Open the bank, then open it again from another banker or chest.
   Check: one icon per marked item (no doubles).


4. A bank the plugin can't read (CUJ 3)
---------------------------------------
This is hard to trigger on purpose. Try depositing and withdrawing several items as
fast as you can.
Check: if the console ever shows "Bank: Couldn't read: ...", no icon was on a wrong
item meanwhile, "Couldn't read Bank, so its markers are off until it reads
correctly." appeared at most once (chat with chatAccess, else the console), and
icons came back once the bank settled ("Bank reads correctly again").
WRITE DOWN every "Couldn't read" line you see, with what you were doing.


5. On/off and shutdown with the bank open
-----------------------------------------
1. With the bank open and a bank tooltip showing, press the shortcut (or untick the
   box).
   Check: every bank, bank backpack and backpack icon and the tooltip go at once.
   Turn markers back on: the icons come back at once.
2. With the bank open and a bank tooltip showing, reload with prl quest-item-marker.
   Check: nothing left on screen from the old run; one icon per marked item after.
3. With a big bank open (most slots used), look for any slowdown while scrolling
   and switching tabs, compared with markers off.


Send back: the console lines from parts 1, 3 and 4, anything that lingered or landed
on the wrong item (with the step), and anything that felt slow.
