Quest Item Marker - M5 (quest type filter) in-game test
=======================================================

What M5 covers: the settings panel's "Mark items needed for" checkboxes, Quests and
Miniquests, both on by default. Turning one off removes the icons and tooltip lines
for that type in every open screen at once, and the choice is kept after a reload or
client restart (CUJ 6).

Automated checks (no client): from the plugin folder run

    lua tests/run.lua

It should end with "83 passed, 0 failed". Those tests use a fake client, so they
don't prove anything about the real one. That's what the steps below are for.

Write down what you see at each "Check". The plugin prints nothing to chat or the
console (caught errors only with Log.DEV = true in src/log.lua).


0. Set up
---------
1. Reload with prl quest-item-marker (prettyui-dev must be on).
2. Have in your backpack (or bank):
   - an item needed only by an unfinished MINIQUEST (e.g. a bar for Doric's Task V,
     if you haven't done it; its tooltip says "(miniquest)"),
   - an item needed only by an unfinished quest (e.g. rune essence for Priest in
     Peril),
   - an item needed by both kinds, if you have one.
3. Open the plugin's ribbon panel.
   Check: under the title there is "Mark items needed for" with "Quests" and
   "Miniquests", both ticked. There is no "Show quest item markers" checkbox.
4. With markers hidden by the shortcut, clear the shortcut field.
   Check: the markers come back (with no shortcut, nothing else could show them).


1. Turning a type off and on (CUJ 6)
------------------------------------
1. Open the bank too, so two screens are open. Untick "Miniquests".
   Check: at once (no half-second wait), the miniquest-only item loses its icon in the
   backpack AND the bank; the quest item keeps its icon. An item needed by both
   keeps its icon, and its tooltip no longer lists the miniquest.
2. Tick "Miniquests" again.
   Check: the icons and tooltip lines come back at once.
3. Untick "Quests" (keep Miniquests on).
   Check: only miniquest items keep icons.
4. Untick both.
   Check: no icons anywhere. Tick both again.
5. With a shop open (one selling a marked item), repeat step 1.
   Check: the shop icons follow too.


2. Kept after reload
--------------------
1. Untick "Miniquests". Reload with prl quest-item-marker.
   Check: the panel shows Miniquests unticked, and the miniquest-only item has no
   icon after login/reload.
2. Restart the client and log in.
   Check: still unticked. Tick it again at the end.


3. No extra work
----------------
1. Untick and tick "Miniquests" ten times quickly, then press the shortcut to hide
   and show markers once.
   Check: no stutter, and icons are right after each change (the filter never reads
   quest status; the unit tests count the reads).


Send back: anything that didn't update at once, and anything that wasn't kept after
the reload or restart.
